# Shop name candidates, round 2

Checked 2026-10-28 by the marketer.

**Criteria (from Shaky):** the name must not box the shop into one product type (no calendar, planner, sign, tag or certificate words). It should suggest useful, well-made paper and digital goods for people's projects, hobbies and small businesses, with room to grow.

All 24 names are 20 characters or fewer, letters only, CamelCase, with no "Etsy", "Canva", "Print My Style", brand, character or celebrity names, and no claims. None repeats a round-1 name in `research/name-ideas.md`.

**Conflict check method:** one web search per name. Names with a strong conflict were replaced and searched again. "Clear" means nothing similar came up. "No exact match" means no business with this name, but similar names are listed. This is not a trademark clearance. Before Shaky picks a name, run the free USPTO trademark search and check the Etsy shop name by hand. The team does not fetch Etsy pages (playbook §3b).

★ = top 5 overall.
