# Seasonal keywords (Team Moneta)

_Shaky asked for two keyword lists: this one for seasonal products, and `keywords-evergreen.md` for year-round products. This is the Researcher's original list from 2026-10-27, mostly holiday phrases. Rows #11 and #22 are evergreen and are copied into the evergreen list. Seasons further ahead are at the bottom._

**How demand was judged (read this first, the signal is thin):**
1. Signals found via web search only: blog lists of popular/underserved digital products (all seller-marketing blogs, no verified sales data), Etsy listings surfacing in search results (several near-identical Christmas gift/budget trackers at about $1-$9, and several Canva-style gift certificate bundles; no sales counts visible), and competing sellers on other marketplaces (Gumroad/Payhip) selling craft-fair vendor kits at about $6-$11.
2. Etsy.com returns 403 to direct fetches, so NO listing counts, review counts, or search-volume numbers were checked for any phrase below. Every "competition guess" is a judgment call from how crowded the category looked, not data. Gumroad voucher templates aimed at salons/spas showed 0 sales, which could mean weak demand for those phrases or just a weak platform for them: unknown.
3. Seasonality (Nov-Dec gifting, New Year planning) is common-sense reasoning plus Canva's own claim that gift certificates boost holiday sales (vendor claim). Before building, Shaky (via the Manager) can spot-check any starred phrase in Etsy search (autocomplete + result count) as a 2-minute sanity test.

**Constraints applied:** no brand, trademark, character, celebrity, or team names. Caveats: (a) "canva" appears in the canva-type phrases because that is how buyers type it and it matches the manager's example shape; it is a trademark, so the Reviewer should decide; the brand-free fallback is "editable template" (already used in some rows). (b) I avoided "Small Business Saturday" and "Shop Small" (trademark-associated) and used "local market" / "holiday sale" instead. (c) Etsy's Oct 22, 2026 AI rules ban AI-generated functional instructions (recipes, patterns, manuals), so no recipe/pattern products are listed. Blank logs and trackers are fine but must be honestly described and, if AI-assisted, disclosed (see verification-notes.md, item e).

Timing note: today is 2026-10-27. New listings take time to be indexed/ranked; gift-certificate phrases peak roughly Nov 15 - Dec 20, planner phrases roughly Dec 1 - Jan 15. Speed matters more than polish.

★ = top 15 pick (low-to-medium competition guess, clear seasonal trigger, easy to produce with no per-order work).
