# Shop name candidates for the rebrand

Checked 2026-10-27 by the marketer. All 24 names are 20 characters or fewer, letters only, CamelCase, with no "Etsy", "Canva", "Print My Style" or brand words.

**Conflict check method:** one web search per name. "clear" means the search found no business using that exact name. This is not a trademark clearance. Before Shaky picks a name, check the top choices in the free USPTO trademark search and by hand on Etsy.

★ = top 5 overall.

## Dropped during checks (replaced)

- **CalmHomeLedger:** "Calm" is a well-known meditation app brand, and Calm Accounts is a bookkeeping firm. Rule 7 risk.
- **MakerMarkDesigns:** too close to "Maker's Mark", a bourbon brand (general knowledge, not confirmed by this search). Also close to the Etsy shop MakeMarksDesign.
- **HomeBinderCo:** HomeBinder is an active homeownership company.
- **CozyCalendarCo:** CozyCalendarClub is an Etsy seller of digital calendar downloads.

Replacements: CraftDeskCo, QuietHomePapers, HomeRoutineCo and EveryDayPlanCo, each searched.
