## Positioning (manager's decision, for Shaky to confirm)

**One audience, two formats:** we make templates and printables for **small businesses, makers and service pros**, such as groomers, cleaners, beauty pros, home bakers and craft-fair vendors.

- **Main line: editable Canva templates.** Gift certificates, price lists, signs, order forms, intake forms, loyalty cards, thank-you inserts. Each one is made for a specific trade.
- **Second line: business printables.** Vendor checklists, inventory and sales logs, content calendars, income, expense and mileage logs.
- **Seasonal versions of both** for each holiday (Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year, Valentine's, Mother's Day, and so on), so the catalog grows every season.

**Why this focus:**
- **Price fits our minimum.** Business templates sell above our $3.99 minimum more often than home printables. Home printables mostly sell for $1–$3, which leaves almost nothing after Mexico fees and the 20% withholding.
- **Narrow niches are the only advice every source agrees on** for a new shop. "Dog groomer gift certificate" beats "gift certificate".
- **Everything we make serves the same buyer.** That keeps the shop coherent and makes it easy to sell bundles and repeat to the same customers.
- **Our holidays are earlier than shoppers' holidays.** Businesses buy templates 2–4 weeks before consumers shop, so a business-focused shop can still catch Christmas.

**Parked:** consumer home and holiday printables, such as Christmas card trackers and chore charts. The visible demand is big, but prices are below our minimum and the market belongs to entrenched shops. We'll revisit with bundles once we have reviews.

## Rename facts (Etsy help, confirmed)
- **Where:** Shop Manager → Settings → Your shop → Shop name → Edit.
- **How many times:** 5 changes on your own. After that, Etsy Support must approve each change.
- **Name rules:** 4–20 characters, no spaces or punctuation.
- **Old links redirect automatically**, and the old name stays reserved for us. For 45 days, a "name changed" icon shows next to the shop name.
- Reviews and sales history most likely stay (third-party sources; Etsy doesn't say).

## Brand kit (built once the name is chosen)
- **Look:** clean and friendly. One warm main colour plus a neutral and an accent, two free Canva fonts (a display font and a body font), and a simple logo built from the name (for TillAndTag, a tag shape). Everything uses free Canva elements.
- **Shop assets:** logo/icon (500×500), banner, shop title (up to 55 characters), announcement, About section, FAQ, and a short policy text for digital items.
- **Shop sections:** Gift Certificates · Price Lists & Menus · Signs & Tags · Forms & Order Sheets · Business Printables · Seasonal.
- **Listing look:** every hero image uses the same layout, colours and corner badge, e.g. "Editable in Canva", so the shop looks like one brand.
- **Voice:** plain, helpful, no hype. Every listing carries the AI disclosure line.
